# Figure Captions

## Figure 1 — Spectral response versus ξ

Mean filtered ΔP/P as a function of the coupling parameter ξ.

Error bars represent the standard deviation across random seeds.

The approximately linear trend indicates that the spectral response scales proportionally with ξ.

---

## Figure 2 — Local signal versus ξ

Mean local CFC correction amplitude as a function of ξ.

The signal increases linearly throughout the explored parameter range.

---

## Figure 3 — Log-log scaling relation

Log-log representation of signal amplitude versus ξ.

A slope close to unity indicates linear scaling.

---

## Figure 4 — Void population stability

Mean detected void count as a function of ξ.

The constant void count suggests that ξ modifies signal amplitude without significantly altering the underlying structure.

---

## Figure 5 — Correction standard deviation versus ξ

Mean correction standard deviation as a function of ξ.

The observed scaling is consistent with a linear dependence on the coupling parameter.
