Figure 1 — Resolution-aware Delta P/P convergence

Mean filtered ΔP/P as a function of grid resolution for ξ = 0.05.

Error bars represent the standard deviation across random seeds.

The increasing signal indicates that the CFC correction remains measurable at higher resolutions and does not disappear under refinement.

Figure 2 — Resolution-aware local correction convergence

Mean local CFC correction amplitude as a function of grid resolution.

The relative stability of the signal suggests improved convergence of local quantities compared with global spectral observables.

Figure 3 — Void count convergence

Mean number of detected voids as a function of grid resolution.

Higher resolutions resolve additional structures and naturally increase the number of identified void regions.

Figure 4 — Example filtered power-spectrum comparison

Comparison between baseline and CFC-corrected power spectra after applying the k-filter.

Only scales below the selected k-threshold are included in order to reduce numerical noise near the Nyquist limit.

Figure 5 — Example filtered ΔP/P spectrum

Relative power-spectrum difference:

ΔP/P = (P_CFC − P_baseline) / P_baseline

displayed after filtering high-k modes.

This figure highlights the scale dependence of the CFC correction.
